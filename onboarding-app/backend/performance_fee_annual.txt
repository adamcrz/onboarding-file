
The Asset Manager is entitled to an annual performance fee of 10% p.a. (plus the applicable value-added tax) on the net capital appreciation. Net capital appreciation is calculated as the increase in the value of the portfolio, taking into account contributions and withdrawals as well as interest and dividend income, less the asset management fee, bank charges, taxes, and any other levies. The performance fee is calculated annually as of 30 November, using a high-water mark methodology.

The 10% performance fee is charged only on the portion of the net capital appreciation up to a maximum annual return of 10% above the previously applicable high-water mark. Any capital appreciation exceeding this 10% annual threshold is not subject to the performance fee.
